# Privacy Policy – GPT-Girl ChatGPT

This extension does not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

- The extension does not log, transmit, or share the content of your chats or any other browsing data.
- It does not use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking.
- All logic runs locally in your browser.

What the extension does:

- It observes the ChatGPT page state (idle / thinking / answering) only to update the background animation.
- It fetches static image and audio files from cdn.jsdelivr.net to display the GPT-Girl animation and play the lo-fi music.

No information is sent to the developer or to any third party, other than the standard HTTP requests needed to load those static assets.

If this policy changes in the future, this page will be updated.
